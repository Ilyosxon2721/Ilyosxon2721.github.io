Bumtap — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026.

Bumtap is a game for toddlers and young children for Android and iOS: fireworks, bubbles, drums, drawing, colors, shapes, pairs and shadows.

We do not collect any data. The game has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no in-app purchases and no links to websites or social networks. It does not use the internet and works fully offline.

Data stored on the device. Only two settings are saved, and only on your device: the interface language and whether sound is on. They are never sent anywhere and are deleted when you delete the game.

Children. Bumtap is made for children. Because the game collects nothing, no personal information about a child is collected, used or shared.

Changes. If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
